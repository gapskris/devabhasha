### Concept 1: The Banyan Tree of Languages (भाषा-पारिजात)
![1. The Banyan Tree of Languages](/C:/Users/gkpan/.gemini/antigravity/brain/dd0693b4-ed17-4f99-aa84-c2130c23753e/devabhasha_tree_languages_1791118433407.jpg)

* **Theme**: Sanskrit as the Mother of Languages (Chapters 1 & 8).
* **Visual Elements**:
  * An illuminated golden **Kalpavriksha** (sacred banyan tree of speech).
  * Its deep roots are formed from golden Sanskrit syllables emerging from the fertile soil of Indian wisdom.
  * Its glowing canopy blossoms with leaves and fruits bearing the letters of the Sanskrit alphabet (*Varṇamālā*).
  * Warm sandalwood paper texture blending into a celestial indigo sky with gold stardust.

### Concept 2: The Sacred Conch of Primordial Sound (नाद-शङ्ख)
![2. The Sacred Conch of Primordial Vibration](/C:/Users/gkpan/.gemini/antigravity/brain/dd0693b4-ed17-4f99-aa84-c2130c23753e/devabhasha_sacred_shankha_1791118459842.jpg)

* **Theme**: The Acoustic Science of Speech & Phonetics (*Śikṣā* / Chapter 2).
* **Visual Elements**:
  * A ceremonial pearl-white and 24k gold jewel-inlaid **Śaṅkha** (conch).
  * Luminous golden waves of sound (*Nāda-Brahma*) flow outwards from the spiral, carrying floating Sanskrit vowels and consonants (*अ, आ, इ, ई, क, ख, ग, घ*) as glowing light particles.
  * Perfect representation of the acoustic vibration of Sanskrit speech.
