16B,Vatsyayana
The author of the great Kamasutra. His date is uncertain, but he appears to have been earlier than Kalidasa and is generally assigned to different periods from third century A.D. to the sixth. According to some, he flourished about the fourth century A.D. Supposed by some to have belonged to Pataliputra; according to others, he belonged to Avanti.
